# Episode 4/5 brief — six AI-generated strategies through the Gauntlet

Written 2026-11-06. Everything here is measured, not assumed.

## What was done

Six strategies were generated by Claude in sealed chats — no project, no
memory, no data — from six different prompts, and put through the gates
below on 21 OANDA CFD markets, daily bars, 2005-01 to 2026-11.

## The gates

```
1 SIGN CONSISTENCY   same sign of edge in all three eras
                     E1 2005-01-01 to 2012-03-31
                     E2 2012-04-01 to 2019-06-30
                     E3 2019-07-01 to 2026-08-31
2 BREADTH            same sign in at least 14 of 21 markets
3 EFFECT SIZE        at least 0.2% per trade, after costs
4 SAMPLE             at least 200 trades
5 ROBUSTNESS         at least 80% of the edge survives moving every
                     parameter by +/-25% of its own value
6 MATCHED CONTROL    measured against bars matched on era, prior return
                     and volatility, held the same number of bars and
                     charged the same costs -- not against doing nothing
7 DAILY RISK         drawdown measured at daily resolution with open
                     positions marked, not at the resolution the strategy
                     chooses to report
```

## Costs, measured from OANDA's own bid and ask streams

both directions. This is the OANDA markup convention.

are unsatisfiable for a two-instrument intraday strategy and were reported
rather than gated.

## The finding

**All six found a positive gross edge. Only the short-hold ones kept it.**

```
          gross    cost      net     hold
  ARM A  +0.303%  0.422%  -0.119%   33 nights
  ARM E  +0.532%  0.624%  -0.091%   28 nights
  ARM B  +0.212%  0.303%  -0.091%   27 nights
  ARM D  +0.329%  0.316%  +0.013%    7 nights
  ARM F  +0.581%  0.058%  +0.523%    4 nights
```

Financing at 2.5% a year is 0.21% a month. A strategy holding a month pays
it every month, and none of the four trend arms earns that back.

**Second pattern: 2012.** Every trend arm is positive in era 1 and negative
in era 2 — A +0.50%/−0.45%, B +0.24%/−0.29%, D +0.52%/−0.38%,
E +0.96%/−0.73%. ARM F's dip-buy is the only thing positive in all three,
decaying gently (+0.58%/+0.52%/+0.46%) rather than flipping.

**Where the survivor is weak.** ARM F part A failed robustness: moving the
RSI entry from 20 to 25 leaves 78% of the edge and the exit from 70 to 52
leaves 72%, against an 80% bar, while every other parameter sits at
87-106%. The edge lives in the RSI thresholds specifically. It also trades
three correlated indices with 54% of entries on the same day as another,
and it is long-only.

**Ten weeks of genuinely unseen data** (2026-08-29 to 2026-11-06) gave ARM
F +0.020%/trade on 13 trades — about 4% of the backtest. Thirteen trades
concludes nothing, but it does not encourage either.
